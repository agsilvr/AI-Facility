## Profit arithmetic

Contribution per order = collected revenue (including charged shipping, excluding tax collected for remittance) minus production, supplier shipping, percentage and fixed platform/payment fees, applicable advertising/acquisition costs, expected returns/replacements and per-order labor.

Required monthly orders = ceiling((monthly profit target + monthly overhead/other cost provision) / positive contribution per order).

Illustration only: $10 contribution per order with $500 monthly overhead requires 1,050 orders to leave $10,000 after those entered costs. This is not a shop forecast. Enter every relevant cost in Command's scenario calculator; it does not save a price or authorize a product.
