# First-listing niche decision — 30 October 2026

Research retrieved 30 October 2026. Scope: Etsy organic search, Printify POD, English-language US buyer hypothesis. No social media. Public web results can be cached and localised; these are observed source snapshots, not logged-in live shop analytics.

## Decision

Prepare **an original bookish-cat crewneck for adult readers, book-club members and librarian gifts** first. Use a refined graphic with strong visual hierarchy rather than another generic winter illustration. This has concrete adjacent item-level purchase evidence and avoids per-order photo proofs during the first launch. It is an evidence-backed first test, **not a guaranteed profitable or low-competition niche**.

Keep **personalised pet keepsake ornaments** as the second candidate: they have the strongest current seasonal trend signal, but require a dependable custom-order process and introduce a much smaller print area. A generic ornament would not earn the same demand claim as personalised pet gifts.

## Creative brief for first listing

Buyer: an adult reader who also loves cats; gift buyer shopping for a librarian, reading friend or book-club member. Occasion: everyday gift and winter reading, with no dated holiday dependence.

Proposed original design: **After Hours Reading Club**. A confident black cat librarian sitting upright behind an open book, framed by a restrained arched bookshelf. Clear silhouette, warm ivory with muted terracotta and dark forest accents, generous breathing space, bespoke bookplate layout and high-contrast lettering. One central scene; no collage, tiny narrative clutter, pseudo-watercolour haze or random decorative filler. Preserve the cat's face and book at thumbnail size. Use crisp serif type placed in code/vector when needed for spelling and alignment. Do not copy the reference listings' artwork, slogan, layout or mockup.

Design review criteria: exact readable wording; coherent anatomy; readable eyes/book; no muddy edges; strong contrast on the selected garment; print-safe line weights at actual physical size; no unintended background/checkerboard; no trademarks/fandom; attractive primary mockup at mobile thumbnail size. A self-graded model score is not enough: inspect the actual exported file and mockup.

Candidate initial colour range: sand and sport grey only if the actual ink contrast and provider availability pass. Use a selected real provider and fewer variants for a clean first launch. Do not claim embroidery when the method is DTG. Do not promise US manufacture merely because fulfillment is in the US.
